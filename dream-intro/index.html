You have had this dream before.

You don't remember when it began.

But lately, you remember more.

At first, there was only the sound.

Touch the darkness.

Towers. Windmills. Streets you somehow recognise. You have never been here. You know exactly where you are.

Move through the memory.

You always reach this part.

Every night you watch it die.

And then you wake.

At least...

you always did.

But tonight...

the dream does not end.

There are footsteps where there were none before.

Someone is standing beside the crystal.

No.

Not someone.

Five.

You thought the dream belonged to you.

It doesn't.
