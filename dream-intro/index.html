You have had this dream before.

You don't remember when it began.

At first, there was almost nothing to remember.

But each night, something remains.

A sound. A shape. A place.

And lately, the edges have begun to sharpen.

The sound is always the first thing you remember.

Touch the darkness.

Then comes the rain.

At first, you only heard it.

Now you can feel each drop against your skin.

Cold. Real.

Sometimes you wake before the rain stops.

And still feel water running down your face.

Your sheets are dry.

Towers.Windmills.Windows glowing through the rain.Streets you somehow recognise.A corner you remember turning.A city becoming clearer every night.

Move through the memory.

This part is clearer than it used to be.

Every night, you remember a little more of how it breaks.

There was a time when the dream ended here.

Or perhaps...

this is simply all you could remember.

Lately, more has begun to remain.

Shapes beyond the dark.

Details that were not there before.

Or details you were not yet able to see.

There is movement beyond the crystal.

You used to mistake it for shadow.

Now you can hear footsteps.

Now you can make out a shape.

Then another.

Five figures, where once there was only darkness.

You cannot see their faces.

Not yet.

But each night, they seem a little closer.
