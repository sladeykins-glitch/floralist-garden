Wipe away what time has hidden

Thy promised death, stolen by thine own hand,
hath denied thee the grave thou wert sworn to claim.

A covenant of death eternal,
whose sweet and solemn rest hath been withheld from thee yet longer.

Let the seed take root within thy heart,

and suffer it to grow, that which was planted long ago.

Let it console you as you place it at the centre.

Arise, I say, and serve once more.

For the hour hath come,
the vow remaineth unbroken,
and the promise, long awaited,
shall at last be fulfilled.

Arise, Sir Isen Willowcrest.

Arise from thy silence.
Arise from thy rest.
And take thy place once more among the living.

The words beneath the moss stand revealed.
